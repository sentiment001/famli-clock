Social Security wage base 2027 and the Maryland FAMLI cap

The 2027 Social Security wage base is $192,300, published by SSA on 14 October 2026. Maryland FAMLI uses the same number: contributions stop once an employee's 2027 wages from one employer pass it.

This page is about Maryland FAMLI, administered by the Maryland Department of Labor, FAMLI Division. Colorado runs a separate paid leave program with the same name and different rules; nothing here applies to it.

The 2027 figure

For wages paid in 2027, the Social Security contribution and benefit base, also called the taxable maximum, is $192,300. SSA, contribution and benefit base SSA announced it on 14 October 2026, with the 2027 cost-of-living adjustment. SSA 2027 COLA fact sheet Social Security tax is 6.2 percent each for employee and employer on wages up to that figure, and nothing above it.

How the wage base has moved

SSA sets the base each year from changes in the national average wage index. In a few years it didn't move at all. These are SSA's own figures, and we don't project future years.

What it caps for Maryland FAMLI

Maryland FAMLI uses SSA's number directly. All wages an employer pays an employee count for contributions up to the Social Security wage base each calendar year. COMAR 09.42.02.04A For 2027 that means FAMLI stops on the same $192,300 that Social Security does.

Each higher earner is assumed to be paid evenly through the year, and everyone else shares the rest of the payroll evenly. These are estimates, not a filing.

From the third quarter, contributions drop: the two higher earners cross the cap and stop counting. That's why spreading the payroll evenly, which is the calculator's default, can overstate the bill when some people earn above $192,300. “Make this exact” in the calculator fixes that.

Two employers, two caps

The Maryland Department of Labor works the cap out separately for each job. FAMLI contributions FAQ, question 19 Someone with two Maryland employers can have contributions taken on wages up to $192,300 at each one. Each employer counts only the wages it pays, so neither one stops early because of the other job.

The cap also resets every 1 January. Wages paid in December 2027 count toward 2027. Wages paid on 1 January 2028 start a new year, under whatever base SSA sets for 2028.

Where the cap does not apply

Medicare. Since 1994 there has been no limit on wages taxed for Medicare's hospital insurance, at 1.45 percent each for employee and employer. SSA, contribution and benefit base So an employee paid $250,000 pays Medicare on all of it, and Social Security and FAMLI on the first $192,300.

The base caps wages, not the FAMLI rate. The 2027 rate is 0.9 percent. The law lets the Secretary raise it to as much as 1.2 percent of wages up to the base, and the 2028 rate is due by 1 November 2027. LE 8.3-601

When it changes next

SSA sets a new figure each year. The 2027 figure came out on 14 October 2026. The calculator and every page on this site use it, and we update them when SSA publishes the 2028 figure.

Questions

What is the Social Security wage base for 2027?

$192,300, published by SSA on 14 October 2026. Social Security tax applies to wages up to that amount, and Maryland FAMLI uses the same cap.

What is the most Maryland FAMLI can take for one employee in 2027?

$1,730.70 at an employer with 15 or more employees, $865.35 each side if the employer withholds the employee half. With fewer than 15, it is $865.35, all of it the employee half.

Does the FAMLI wage cap apply per employer?

Yes. The Department works out the cap separately for each job, so someone with two employers can have contributions on wages up to the cap at each.

Is there a wage base cap on Medicare tax?

No. There has been no limit on Medicare hospital insurance wages since 1994. The 1.45 percent applies to all wages.
