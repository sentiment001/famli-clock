Maryland sick and safe leave vs FAMLI

Maryland employers now run two leave laws that sound alike. Sick and safe leave, under the Healthy Working Families Act, has applied since February 2018 and the employer pays for it. FAMLI is insurance: contributions start on wages paid from 1 January 2027, and benefits from January 2028.

This page is about Maryland FAMLI, administered by the Maryland Department of Labor, FAMLI Division. Colorado runs a separate paid leave program with the same name and different rules; nothing here applies to it.

The short version

Sick and safe leave is time off the employer gives and pays for itself, earned an hour at a time. FAMLI is insurance. Employers and employees pay into a state fund, and the fund, or an approved private plan, pays the employee during longer leave. The Department's own example: sick days are for an everyday cold, and FAMLI is for a serious illness. FAMLI FAQ, question 15

Both cover almost every employer with Maryland staff, so you will usually owe both. One doesn't replace the other.

The two 15-employee lines are not the same line

Both laws split employers at 15, and they count in different ways. For sick and safe leave the number is the average monthly headcount over the previous year, LE 3-1304 and the Commissioner looks only at people employed in Maryland. MD Labor sick and safe leave FAQ For FAMLI it is everyone under the federal EIN, inside and outside Maryland, decided each quarter during 2027. FAMLI FAQ, question 26

Take a firm with 10 people in its Maryland office and 30 more in Virginia. For sick and safe leave it is under 15, so unpaid leave meets the law. For FAMLI it has 40 employees and pays the full 0.9 percent. On a $750,000 Maryland payroll that is $6,750.00 for 2027, half of it employer money. An employer that sizes itself once and applies the answer to both laws gets one of them wrong.

Can employees use sick days during FAMLI leave?

The employer can't make them. The law stops employers from requiring employees to use paid time off, including sick days and vacation, before or alongside FAMLI. An employer can require unpaid leave, such as federal FMLA leave, to run at the same time. FAMLI FAQ, questions 116 and 117

The employee can choose to. If the employer allows it, an employee can use accrued leave to top up the FAMLI benefit to 100 percent of wages, but only if the employee agrees. question 122 An employee can also skip FAMLI entirely for a qualifying event and use their own leave instead. Nobody has to file a claim. question 118

Employer parental leave is treated differently. Leave designed for a FAMLI purpose, which the Department calls Alternative FAMLI Purpose Leave, can be required to run at the same time as FAMLI, and those weeks count toward the 12. questions 119 and 120

What changes on the pay stub in January 2027

Sick and safe leave already requires a statement of used and available leave with each pay. MD Labor sick and safe leave FAQ That stays. What's new is a FAMLI deduction, if you choose to withhold, on wages paid from 1 January 2027. FAMLI FAQ, question 8 Employees must get written notice at least one pay period before it first appears. COMAR 09.42.02.05D If you cover the employee half yourself, there is no deduction to show.

The FAMLI quarterly wage and hour report is new too, and it is separate from your unemployment insurance filing in BEACON. It reports gross wages. FAMLI FAQ, questions 33 and 37

Which one applies

A two-day flu is sick and safe leave. Twelve weeks after surgery or a birth, from 2028, is FAMLI, perhaps with sick days topping up pay if you allow it and the employee agrees. Time off after domestic violence is sick and safe leave: FAMLI does not cover it. FAMLI FAQ, question 70

Questions

Is FAMLI the same as Maryland sick and safe leave?

No. Sick and safe leave is leave the employer provides and pays for directly. FAMLI is insurance, funded by contributions from 1 January 2027 and paying benefits from January 2028.

Do employers with fewer than 15 employees have to give sick and safe leave?

Yes, but it can be unpaid. The count is the average monthly headcount in the previous year, and the Commissioner counts Maryland employees only.

Can an employer require employees to use sick leave during FAMLI leave?

No. Employers can't require employees to use paid time off, sick days included, with FAMLI. They may allow employees to top up the benefit to full pay with accrued leave, if the employee agrees.

Does the 15-employee count work the same way for both laws?

No. Sick and safe leave uses last year's average monthly Maryland headcount. FAMLI counts everyone under the EIN in every state, quarter by quarter during 2027.
